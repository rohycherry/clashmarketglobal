Refund Policy

We want transactions on Clash Market Global (CMG) to be as clear and smooth as possible. This page explains how refunds are handled.

Transaction Payments

Payments made between buyers and sellers are part of a direct transaction between the users. Clash Market Global does not automatically provide refunds for completed transactions.

Middleman Transactions

If a transaction uses our middleman service, please contact the middleman as soon as possible if there is a problem. The situation may be reviewed based on the available transaction information.

Before Completing a Trade

Please carefully check the account details, price, payment information, and other important details before completing a transaction.

Completed Transactions

Once a transaction has been completed and the parties have received what they agreed to, refunds are generally not available.

Problems or Disputes

If you believe there has been a problem with a transaction, contact support and provide the relevant details. We will try to understand the situation and assist where possible.

Contact Us

For refund or transaction questions, please contact us at rohycherry@clashmarketglobal.com .
