Knowledge Base

Welcome to the Clash Market Global Knowledge Base. Here you can find simple information and helpful tips about using our platform and trading Clash of Clans accounts.

What is Clash Market Global?

Clash Market Global (CMG) is a community platform where users can connect, share account listings, and discuss Clash of Clans account trading.

Buying an Account

If you are looking to buy an account, take your time and check the listing carefully. Make sure the account information matches what the seller has provided before completing a trade.

Selling an Account

Sellers should provide clear and honest information about their account. Adding useful details helps buyers understand what they are considering.

Using a Middleman

A middleman can help coordinate a transaction between a buyer and seller. The middleman helps keep the process organized and can reduce common risks during a trade.

Stay Safe

When trading online, it is always a good idea to be careful. Some simple safety tips include:

  • Check the other person's information before trading.
  • Do not share unnecessary personal information.
  • Be careful with unusual payment requests.
  • Keep records of important transaction details.
  • Use the official middleman when appropriate.
