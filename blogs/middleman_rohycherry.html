Understanding the Role of a Middleman

Learn how a neutral third party can help coordinate transactions between buyers and sellers and why verification remains important.

When two people who do not know each other want to complete a digital transaction, the biggest challenge is usually trust. A buyer wants assurance about what they are receiving, while a seller wants assurance that agreed payment conditions will be completed. A middleman can help coordinate communication between both parties.

What Is a Middleman?

A middleman is a neutral third party who helps coordinate a transaction between a buyer and seller. Instead of requiring one person to completely trust the other, both parties follow an agreed process with assistance from the third party.

How Does the Process Work?

1

Agreement

The buyer and seller discuss the item, price, and terms before beginning the transaction process.

2

Coordination

The middleman helps coordinate communication and the agreed transaction process between both parties.

3

Completion

Once the agreed conditions have been completed and confirmed, the transaction can move toward final completion.

Why Verification Matters

Online impersonation is one of the biggest risks in digital communities. Someone may copy a username, profile picture, or reputation to appear trustworthy.

!
A Middleman Does Not Remove All Risk

A middleman can help coordinate a process, but users should still understand the transaction and verify contacts independently.
